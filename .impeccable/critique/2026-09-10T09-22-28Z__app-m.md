# Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The bottom-tab shell itself is a faithful, well-executed implementation of the project's own `08-pwa.md` redesign spec (correct 76px bar, 2px top rule, 52px central "+" in the brand color). But the redesign is not realized past the nav layer. The "R$" tab opens a 6-link hub (Despesas/Receitas/Relatórios/Fluxo de Caixa/DRE/Livro Caixa) — the exact "peso morto" the spec's own diagnosis names for removal. "Novo atendimento," which the spec calls "a tela que justifica o PWA" (three fields + optional dictation), is instead a 10+ field form with fee-mode toggles, a pendency checklist, and drag-and-drop uploads — no `SpeechRecognition` call exists anywhere in the repo. `relatorios`, `configuracoes`, and `contatos` were meant to leave `/m` entirely; live navigation confirms all three still render fully, unguarded, at their original URLs. Code comments attribute this to a deliberate owner decision to keep everything reachable via the avatar menu — a real, acknowledged scope reversal, not an oversight, but one where the nav chrome was updated as if the leaner shell had shipped when three of five target screens were not actually cut down.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect --json app/m components/mobile` (exit 2) — 130 findings across 40 files: 121 `design-system-font-size` (literal off-ramp sizes, mostly 9–11px), 8 `design-system-radius`, and 1 `warning`-severity `side-tab` hit (`app/m/publicacoes/page.tsx:97`, a `border-l-4` colored card edge — the tool's own documentation calls this "the most recognizable tell of AI-generated UIs").

**Visual overlays (browser evidence):** Live injection on `/m/publicacoes` found **387** anti-patterns on that one screen alone — the static single `side-tab` hit is actually a per-publication-row runtime pattern (`nested-cards` + `border-l-4` accent), multiplied across ~2,673 unread items. `/m/agenda` and `/m/financeiro` each showed a smaller, consistent set: `undersized-ui-text` on the office name (10px), the "99+" badge (9px), and all 4 bottom-nav labels (10px) — below even the tool's 11px floor, let alone `08-pwa.md`'s own stated 13px body-text minimum. Direct-URL navigation confirmed `relatorios`, `configuracoes`, and `contatos` all render completely, with no redirect or auth/role guard blocking them.

# Overall Impression

The shell (bottom nav, central action) is a genuine, well-built piece of the redesign. But the two screens a lawyer would actually reach for under time pressure — logging a new lead and checking the month's numbers — are the two that quietly kept their full desktop complexity, undermining the entire premise ("no corredor do fórum, isso é peso morto") the redesign exists to fix.

# What's Working

- **`components/mobile/MobilePublicationCard.tsx`** — collapsed-by-default triage card with inline expand, "+N outras fontes" grouping, and an undo-toast on mark-as-read — the one screen that gets progressive disclosure right.
- **`app/m/agenda/page.tsx`** — merges tasks and finance due-dates into one day view with distinct left-border colors, answering "what's due today" without a tab switch.
- **`components/mobile/MobileBottomNav.tsx`** — correct 76px/44px-touch-target shell with a sound central "+" entry point, properly deferring qualification/funnel logic to desktop per the spec.

# Priority Issues

**[P0] `/m/financeiro` is the sprawling hub the spec explicitly says to remove, sitting behind the primary "R$" tab.**
Why it matters: `08-pwa.md`'s own diagnosis is "financeiro com sete sub-rotas... isso é peso morto"; its acceptance bar is a 3-number month summary. Tapping the flagship money tab today reproduces the exact problem the redesign exists to fix.
Fix: Build the actual 3-number summary (caixa saldo, contas a vencer hoje, atrasadas) as the `/m/financeiro` landing content; keep the 6 sub-routes one "ver detalhes" tap away, not as the tab's entire content.
Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`

**[P0] "Novo atendimento" is not the 3-field, dictation-assisted flow the spec names as the reason the PWA exists.**
Why it matters: `components/mobile/MobileNewAttendanceForm.tsx` has 10+ fields (fee-mode toggle, pendency editor, drag-and-drop attachments) confirmed live at `/m/atendimento/novo`; zero `SpeechRecognition` calls exist in the repo. This is the one screen meant to let a lawyer log a lead in seconds in a courthouse hallway.
Fix: Ship the spec's minimal path (Nome, Telefone, Assunto with a mic button, Salvar) as the default view; move fee/pendências/anexos behind a "mais detalhes" disclosure, matching the spec's own note that qualification/funnel stays on desktop.
Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`

**[P1] Systemic touch-target violations against the surface's own 44px minimum.**
Why it matters: Agenda day/week paging arrows measure 36px (`h-9 w-9`); header Bell/Menu icons measure 32px — confirmed both in source and via live `getBoundingClientRect`. These are exactly the controls a one-handed user in a hallway reaches for repeatedly.
Fix: Raise these to 44px (`h-11 w-11`) minimum, keeping the icon glyph visually unchanged inside a larger tap area.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] Body text below the spec's own 13px floor is the norm, not the exception (121 CLI hits across 40 files, corroborated live).**
Why it matters: Bottom-nav labels, badge counts, and secondary metadata across `/m` render at 9–11px — office name (10px), "99+" badge (9px), all 4 tab labels (10px) confirmed live. `08-pwa.md` states plainly: "Corpo 15px; nada abaixo de 13px."
Fix: Raise the floor to 13px for anything carrying information; reserve smaller sizes for pure decoration only, if that.
Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

**[P2] The "+" sheet and the atendimento pendency checklist both exceed the ≤4-visible-options guidance at a single decision point.**
Why it matters: `MobileNewEntitySheet.tsx` shows 9 options (4+5) in one modal; the pendency checklist is a flat 6-checkbox list — more simultaneous choices means slower, more error-prone selection exactly where speed matters most.
Fix: Promote "Atendimento" to a larger first tile above the rest of the sheet grid; default-collapse the pendency checklist behind an "adicionar pendência" toggle.
Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

# Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted, one-handed — primary persona for this surface):** Opening the attendance form mid-interruption loses all typed data — no autosave, no confirm-on-back. The 36px agenda paging arrows sit right at the edge of comfortable one-handed reach and are below the spec's own 44px floor.

**Alex (Power User, courthouse time pressure):** The one screen built for Alex's exact job (log a lead, get back to the docket) is now the slowest form in the app — no shortcuts, no dictation despite the spec calling for it. The R$ tab, meant to be glance-and-go, instead makes Alex choose among six sub-screens before seeing a single number.

**Sam (Accessibility):** Icon-only header controls have `aria-label`s (good), but the 32px hit targets fail even the more lenient 24px CSS target-size guidance, compounding the problem for anyone with reduced fine motor control.

# Minor Observations

- `app/m/financeiro/contas-a-pagar/page.tsx` cleanly redirects to `/m/financeiro/despesas` — good practice, worth extending anywhere else a route was renamed.
